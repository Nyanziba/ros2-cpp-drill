Register reads (volatile):
  Read 1: 0x11111111
  Read 2: 0x11111111
  Read 3: 0x11111111

Bit manipulation (volatile):
Initial: 0x00000000
After |= (1 << 3): 0x00000008
After |= (1 << 7): 0x00000088
After &= ~(1 << 3): 0x00000080

Note: volatile does NOT guarantee atomicity!
RMW operations can be interrupted mid-operation.
